# Shop Lab

`tools/shop_lab.html` is a standalone browser page (open the file directly; three.js r128 from CDN, same as `building_lab.html`) over the shared generator `tools/shops.js` (`Shops.build(seed, o)`, also used by `tools/bake_buildings.js` and `tools/level_editor_lab.html`) that generates a roadside car shop / tuning garage from a seed. It models no stock: no cars, bays, bay paint, car labels or price tags, and the yard is open ground.

**Cars are not generated or placed by the lab.** The game parks the shop's real cars itself on the open yard in front of the building (`LevelView.park_stock` / `yard_layout`, from the model's `box`).

**Shop archetypes** (`ARCH` table, chosen by seed, or forced with the Style select / `?style=`): `tin` corrugated workshop with roller doors, `barn` stone farm outbuilding with slate roof and stone chimney, `nissen` curved-vault hut with brick/plain end wall, `showroom` glass-fronted with flat parapet roof, canopy and a side service bay, `cabin` portakabin office with an open steel canopy over the bays, `timber` board-clad shed with an open lean-to and firewood. Seed-driven variation inside them: roof kind (gable, mono-pitch, curved, flat with parapet via `roof()`), roller/barn/personnel doors and windows shuffled along the front (`front()` slots), optional lean-to (`leanTo()`), flue or stack, wall colours. Sign style (fascia board, pole sign at the yard edge, or text painted on the wall) and yard fence (none, wire, chain-link, stone wall, tin sheet, rail, with a pair of galvanised five-bar farm gates swung open towards the road, `farmGate`) are also seeded, as are the forecourt surface (palette concrete, tarmac or gravel) with oil stains, an old pair of petrol pumps (`pumps`) and a floodlight. The `cabin` canopy covers the bays only, leaving the office roof in the open. Unset sign text picks a seeded name, a quarter of them Welsh ("Garej <place>"). Each archetype returns its measurements (width, depth, extents, sign anchors) so pad, bays, parts and fence adapt.

Parts are a fixed generic display set (`DISPLAY_PARTS`, independent of any stock): props (`makePart`: turbo on a pallet, lightweight discs, radiator, rollcage frame, gearbox, nitrous bottles, aero wing on stands) on trestle tables (`display`; the roll cage stands on the ground) in a row along the front wall, with no price tags. Tyre stacks, oil drums and optional bunting (`bunting`: strings from the building's front corners to two flag poles and across the front) dress the yard. The camera reframes the whole shop when the seed, style or size changes (`frame`), but not on sign, palette or bunting changes.

Controls: seed, sign text, style, palette, size, bunting toggle, Copy model JSON (the `{seed, style, pal, size, sign, bunting}` to paste into a level shop's `model`). `?seed=`, `?sign=` and `?style=` preselect. It uses plain flat-coloured Lambert materials (no atlas), so it is a layout/look prototype, not yet the one-mesh baked form. r128 notes: set `mesh.position`/`rotation` via `.set`/assignment (no `Object.assign(mesh, {position})`), and `MeshLambertMaterial` has no `flatShading`.

**Into a level.** A shop in the level save carries only the generator inputs, `model: {x, z, yaw, seed, style, pal, size, sign, bunting}`; `node tools/bake_buildings.js tools/levels/<level>.json` (run from the repo root; needs `npm install` in `tools/` for three@0.128) rebuilds each from them with `{noPad, noText, solid}` and writes it into `<level>.buildings.json` as a `kind: "shop"` entry (`shop` = index in `level.shops`, `x`, `z`, `yaw`, `box` = building bounds `[minx, maxx, minz, maxz, h]`, `texts` = the sign markers, and flat triangles like a building's). `solid` turns the see-through parts opaque for the one-mesh atlas: oil stains are dark decals, glass solid, and the chain-link fence is an alpha-cut diamond cell in the atlas's `chain` strip. Colours are baked as material colour times the sun shading, on the white atlas cell. `LevelBuildings` merges them into the buildings' single mesh (see level-editor.md). Re-bake after changing a shop's `model` or `shops.js`.
