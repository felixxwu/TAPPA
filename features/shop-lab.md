# Shop Lab

`tools/shop_lab.html` is a standalone browser page (open the file directly; three.js r128 from CDN, same as `building_lab.html`) that generates a roadside car shop / tuning garage from a seed and a level-format stock list: `[{"car": "<id>", "price": n} | {"part": "<id>", "price": n}]`.

**Cars are not generated.** The game places its real car models. Each `car` entry gets a marked parking bay on the forecourt instead (painted lines shared between neighbours, a kerb stop, the car id painted on the floor, a price board at its road end), 4.8 x 2.4 m, or 5.8 x 2.7 m for ids matching `f150|beast|truck|pickup|van`. Bays fill rows of up to four, nose to the road; a seed may angle them all (herringbone). The readout box and **Copy bay JSON** button give `[{"car","x","z","yaw","len","wid"}]`: bay centre relative to the shop origin (centre of the building at ground level, front facing +z), yaw in radians about +y, where yaw 0 means the car faces +z (towards the road). The same array is stored in `userData.bays` of the root (so it lands in the .glb extras).

**Shop archetypes** (`ARCH` table, chosen by seed, or forced with the Style select / `?style=`): `tin` corrugated workshop with roller doors, `barn` stone farm outbuilding with slate roof and stone chimney, `nissen` curved-vault hut with brick/plain end wall, `showroom` glass-fronted with flat parapet roof, canopy and a side service bay, `cabin` portakabin office with an open steel canopy over the bays, `timber` board-clad shed with an open lean-to and firewood. Seed-driven variation inside them: roof kind (gable, mono-pitch, curved, flat with parapet via `roof()`), roller/barn/personnel doors and windows shuffled along the front (`front()` slots), optional lean-to (`leanTo()`), flue or stack, wall colours. Sign style (fascia board, pole sign at the yard edge, or text painted on the wall) and yard fence (none, wire, chain-link, stone wall, tin sheet, rail, with a pair of galvanised five-bar farm gates swung open towards the road, `farmGate`) are also seeded, as are the forecourt surface (palette concrete, tarmac or gravel) with oil stains, an old pair of petrol pumps (`pumps`) and a floodlight. The `cabin` canopy covers the bays only, leaving the office roof in the open. Unset sign text picks a seeded name, a quarter of them Welsh ("Garej <place>"). Each archetype returns its measurements (width, depth, extents, sign anchors) so pad, bays, parts and fence adapt.

Each stocked part is a prop (`makePart`: turbo on a pallet, lightweight discs, radiator, rollcage frame, gearbox, nitrous bottles, aero wing on stands) shown on a trestle table with its price tag (`display`; the roll cage stands on the ground) in a row along the front wall, between the doors and the bays. Tyre stacks, oil drums and optional bunting (`bunting`: strings from the building's front corners to two flag poles and across the front) dress the yard. The camera reframes the whole shop when the seed, style, stock or size changes (`frame`), but not on sign, palette or bunting changes.

Controls: seed, stock JSON, sign text, style, palette, size, bunting toggle, bay JSON copy, Download .glb. `?seed=`, `?sign=` and `?style=` preselect. It uses plain flat-coloured Lambert materials (no atlas), so it is a layout/look prototype, not yet the one-mesh baked form. r128 notes: set `mesh.position`/`rotation` via `.set`/assignment (no `Object.assign(mesh, {position})`), and `MeshLambertMaterial` has no `flatShading`.

**Into a level.** `buildShop(..., {noPad: true, noText: true})` leaves the concrete pad out, bakes no text into textures (`textPlane`: each sign, price tag and bay name becomes a marker, returned as `texts` [{text, color, w, h, p, q}] relative to the shop origin, so the model carries no images) (a level gravel patch is the yard) and its `info` returns the yard and building measurements (`padW`, `zBack`, `zFront`, `extL`, `extR`, `bd`, `wh`) used to place the model, its `box` collider and the gravel patch; see the shop `model` field in [level-editor.md](level-editor.md). Exported models live in `models/shops/`.
