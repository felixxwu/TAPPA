# Rallies

A level save's `rallies` list holds named point-to-point rallies on Hafren. Each one has a `start` and a `finish`, written as `{x, z, yaw}`, and a rally end's yaw turns its −z to the way the cars leave the line.

- **Finding spots:** `tools/rally_spots.gd` / `.tscn` scans the level for straight, level road at least 6 m wide, with under 3 m of ground rise across the start area and no junctions, buildings or shops nearby. It pairs the spots into rallies 5–7 km long and names each rally after its nearest teleport point (e.g. "Pen Rhiw Stages").
- **Ground:** every spot gets a 50 × 23 m gravel pad, and `pack_level.js` clears the trees and ground cover off it. The pads are baked into the ground and always show. Rallies are left out of the bake key, so editing a rally never forces a rebake.
- **In game:** `scripts/level_rallies.gd` (`LevelRallies`) shows only the active rally (`LevelRallies.active`; "" means none). `show_active` rebuilds a start line at both ends, a baked variant chosen by `variant_for` (a hash of the rally name and end over `LevelBuildings.start_lines`' seeds). `crowd_groups` hands `LevelSpectators.set_extra` crowds beside each line, kept out of `CONTROL_ZONE` at the start-control end.
- **Baked lines:** there are no `.glb`s. `tools/bake_buildings.js` builds each seed in `StartLines.SEEDS` (`tools/start_lines.js`, 7 m road, 50 m zone) into a `kind: "start_line"` entry of `<level>.buildings.json` (only when the level has rallies; flat colour in vertex colours, sun shading baked, the atlas's white cell for UVs, sign text as `texts`). `LevelBuildings._start_line` turns each into its own one-surface mesh on the buildings' material plus one `ConcavePolygonShape3D` from every triangle above `MIN_SOLID_Y` (arch, tents, fences and tape solid; road paint and mats not), kept in `start_lines`, never in the merged buildings mesh. `line_model` places one: a single mesh (one draw call), a collider, and `LevelBuildings.text_label` labels. Re-run the bake after changing `start_lines.js`.
- **Dev:** the Settings → Dev "Rally: …" button cycles through None and each rally.
- **Tests:** `tests/headless/test_level_rallies.gd`.
