Privacy Policy for TAPPA

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to the TAPPA game ("the game"), a single-player rally driving game for the web, desktop and mobile.

Playing without an account

An account is optional. Without one the game collects, transmits and shares no personal data: it has no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. Your progress (owned cars, upgrades and so on) is saved only on your device, in a file named profile.json, and uninstalling the game or clearing its site data removes it.

Optional account and cloud save

If you choose to sign in, the game uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), operated by Google, to back your progress up and let you continue it on another device. Signing in stores:

  • Your email address, which you enter to receive a one-time sign-in link. It is used only to sign you in and is never used for marketing or shared with anyone else.
  • An account ID, a random identifier Firebase assigns to your account.
  • Your cloud save: a copy of your game progress, stored in Cloud Firestore under your account ID, with the time it was saved and the kind of device that saved it (for example "web" or "android"). Only you, signed in, can read or change it.

On your device the game also keeps a small sign-in file so you stay signed in between sessions. Firebase may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver these services; see Firebase's privacy information.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from inside the game: Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your cloud save and your account, including your email address, from Firebase, and signs you out. If you have not signed in recently, the game first emails you a fresh sign-in link to confirm it is you. Your progress on the device you are using is left untouched. If you can no longer open the game, email us at the address below and we will delete your account for you.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal data from children. The optional account is not intended for children under 13; if you believe a child has created one, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
