# Cold open — the new-player intro

**Status:** planned with Felix on 2026-10-30, not started. Brainstorm and the ideas that were
dropped or parked: the "TAPPA: organic new-player intro" doc in the project thread.

## The idea

A brand-new player doesn't see the title screen first. They start mid-drive at night in an MX-5,
in the chase camera, already dying: the cooling has gone, the bonnet is steaming and Temp is
climbing. Down the road, a lamp post glows by a cabin, and a trail of coins leads to it. They nurse
the car there, picking up coins; the cabin lights, the car heals, the night clears, and the camera
and control options come up. With the trail's money they can keep the MX-5 or buy another starter
car at the cabin. In about a minute they have learned to drive, what
Temp means and that cabins are safety, without a word of tutorial text.

Agreed with Felix:

- **It must be impossible to fail, while feeling desperate.** A health floor stops the car giving
  up, and the road is short with the lamp post in view.
- **No downhill finish** — keep it simple (Felix, 2026-10-30).
- **The beacon is a lamp post at every cabin**, no light above the cabin (Felix, 2026-10-31).
- **Chase camera** for the drive; the camera and control options are offered at the first
  checkpoint (Felix, 2026-10-31).
- **No wrong-way handling** (Felix, 2026-10-31).
- **Always the MX-5**, with **a coin trail to the first checkpoint** worth enough to buy another
  starter car there instead (Felix, 2026-10-31). No "Choose your car" page for a new player.
- Only a brand-new profile gets it. Existing saves boot as today.

## What exists now (verified 2026-10-30)

**First boot.** `scripts/title_boot.gd` → `_ready`: with no world, it makes one
(`WorldSession.new_world(Config.data.free_roam.pick_world_seed(), TitleScreen.starter_choices()[0])`),
sets `WorldSession.fresh = true` and `title_pending = true`, then loads `free_roam.tscn`.
`free_roam.gd` → `_hand_over` sees `title_pending` and calls `show_title()`: the paused world under
an orbit camera (`_start_title_orbit`) and `TitleScreen`. A fresh player then gets
`View.FIRST_RUN` (camera, plus touch layout on a touch device; `TitleScreen.needs_first_run`,
`FIRST_RUN_DONE_KEY`), then `MAIN`, then New World → `STARTER` (pick a car). Picking the car the
fresh world already has just closes the menu (`TitleScreen._start`).

**Spawn.** `free_roam.gd` → `_ready` places the car at `_rest_stops.pose_at(0)`, which for the
spawn site is `Config.data.spawn_ahead_m` down the road out, facing away from the cabin. A new
world starts with site 0 already lit (`WorldSession.new_world` → `"lit": [0]`, `"last_site": 0`).

**Damage chain** (`scripts/damage_model.gd`, `features/damage.md`). `thermal_step` breaks the
cooling once `hp <= max_hp * cfg.cooling_failure_hp_fraction`; while `cooling_broken`,
`engine_temp` only rises (`engine_heat_c_per_s` × rpm fraction); above
`overheat_temp_threshold_c` it drains `overheat_hp_drain_per_s` through `apply_loss`, which emits
`gave_up` at 0 HP. `overheat_ramp` feeds the misfire / rev cap, and `EngineSmoke` steams from the
bonnet. There is no HP floor today; `invulnerable` (used by the benchmark) blocks all loss.

**HUD.** `hud.gd` → `_update_temp` shows the Temp gauge whenever `thermal` is on, and the cooling
label under it: "RADIATOR DAMAGED", then "OVERHEATING — REACH A CHECKPOINT".

**Rest stops** (`scripts/free_roam/rest_stops.gd`). `step` lights a site when the car drives past
it (`rest_reached` → `light`), makes it `last_site`, and `_heal`s while the car stays; a full heal
calls `DamageModel.repair` (cooling fixed). `lit` drives `CheckpointBanner.show_reached` ("Checkpoint
reached") and the loot director. On the first step after a load (`_primed`) a car already on a
site counts as arrived, not as passing. `_on_gave_up` → wreck page → `respawn()` moves to
`last_site`.

**Cabin lamp.** `terrain_streamer.gd` → `refresh_site_marker` hangs the warm `Lamp` (`OmniLight3D`,
`cabin_lamp_energy` / `cabin_lamp_range_m`) only inside a **lit** cabin.

**Weather.** `BiomeDirector.forced_weather` overrides the ring's draw (the dev weather picker
uses it); `""` follows the rings, and a change cross-fades (`_start_fade`). Every ring pool has
`night` in it (`FreeRoamSettings.ring_weather`).

**Roads.** `RoadNetwork.polyline(ei)` gives an edge's points; the network grows out of spawn
(`FreeRoamSettings.roads_from_spawn` edges leave it), with `RoadSites` site 0 beside it. `RoadRecovery.road_pose(streamer, p, facing)` seats a car on the road.

## The plan

### 1. Where it happens: the road into the spawn cabin

No new geometry. The car starts `cold_open_distance_m` (a few hundred metres) down the spawn
cabin's own road, **facing back towards the cabin**, and site 0 is the goal. Walk the spawn edge's
`polyline` from the site-0 end to find the start point and the facing, then `road_pose` it.

- Site 0 starts **unlit** during the cold open (the world is made with `"lit": []`), so arriving
  lights it the normal way: banner, crowd, loot.
- **The beacon is a lamp post** (Felix, 2026-10-31; no light above the cabin). The existing
  interior lamp can't be seen at all at night, not even from 10 m in front of the door
  (`tools/lamp_capture.gd`, 2026-10-31). Replace it with a lantern post at the cabin's front corner:
  a wooden post and arm, an unshaded glowing lamp box, a billboard halo, and an `OmniLight3D`
  (range ~14 m) that lights the cabin front, pad and crowd orange. `tools/lamp_capture.gd -- --lantern`
  builds the mock-up (`_add_lantern`; ignore its overhead glow, which Felix turned down). **Every
  cabin gets one, glowing, lit or not** (Felix, 2026-10-31: night can fall anywhere later in the
  game), so it's built with the cabin in `TerrainStreamer._site_marker`, not in `_light_cabin`'s lit
  branch. That also covers site 0 during the cold open with no special case. Lit vs unlit still
  shows on the minimap (`MiniMap.BEACON_LIT` / `BEACON_UNLIT`). It's one more light per streamed
  cabin, so check the frame cost on the phone build, and keep `shadow_enabled = false`.
- **Line of sight decides the start, not brightness.** At driver height the road's bends and the
  roadside trees hide the cabin from 100 m (the capture's 100 m and 300 m shots). Choose the start
  point so the lantern is in view down the road from it: walk back from the cabin only along the
  straight-enough part of the edge, and stop where the view would turn away.
- Pick the edge that leaves spawn with the fewest bends and no junction within the distance, so
  "no other road to take" holds. If no edge qualifies, shorten the distance to the first junction.

### 2. A small controller: `ColdOpen`

A new node (`scripts/free_roam/cold_open.gd`, added by `free_roam.gd` only when the cold open is
due) that owns the whole sequence, so nothing else grows a cold-open branch:

- **Due when** `WorldSession.fresh` and a new profile flag (e.g. `cold_open_done` on the world
  block, written through `WorldSession`) is unset. Not on Continue, not after New World from the
  title, not under the benchmark or `--auto-drive`.
- **At start:** place the car (step 1); set `damage.cooling_broken = true`, `hp` to
  `cold_open_start_hp_frac × max_hp` (below the cooling threshold), `engine_temp` to
  `cold_open_start_temp_frac` of the overheat threshold; set
  `BiomeDirector.forced_weather = "night"`.
- **Health floor:** a new `DamageModel.min_hp` (default 0) that `apply_loss` won't go below.
  `ColdOpen` sets it to `cold_open_hp_floor_frac × max_hp` and clears it on finish. Overheating
  still pulses, misfires and caps revs (the ramp is temperature-based), so the car limps rather
  than stops. Crash damage hits the same floor, so a player who hits a tree can't wreck either.
- **Camera:** the chase camera for the drive (`CameraManager.Mode.CHASE`; a new profile has no
  saved `camera_mode`, so write it before the camera manager reads it, or set it on the manager).
- **Finish** on `RestStops.lit(0)`: clear `min_hp`, set `forced_weather` to `"dry"` so the night
  cross-fades to day (then back to `""` once the fade is done), mark `cold_open_done`, and once the
  "Checkpoint reached" band has gone, open the first-run options (step 3). Closing them leaves the
  player at the lit cabin, in the game, with the rest-stop prompt up and the trail's money to spend.
  No title menu.

### 3. Boot order and the car

- **The car.** `title_boot.gd` makes a first-time world with `TitleScreen.starter_choices()[0]`
  today. Make the cold-open car a setting instead (`FreeRoamSettings.cold_open_car_id`, shipped as
  the MX-5's id in `game_config.tres`, not a literal in code), falling back to
  `starter_choices()[0]` if the id isn't in `CarLibrary`.
- **No title, no starter page.** `title_boot.gd` sets `title_pending` only when the cold open is
  **not** due. When it is due, `free_roam.gd → _hand_over` starts `ColdOpen` instead of
  `show_title()`, and `WorldSession.fresh` is cleared once the cold open finishes, so a later boot
  is a normal Continue.
- **Options at the first checkpoint.** The first-run pages (`TitleScreen`'s `View.FIRST_RUN`:
  camera, then touch layout on a touch device) move to the end of the cold open. Show them on
  their own over the paused world, without `MAIN` after; "Next" on the last page closes them and
  resumes. That likely means a small host for the FIRST_RUN view outside `show_title()` (or a
  `TitleScreen` flag that closes instead of going to `MAIN`). It's a menu, so it keeps
  `MenuNav.attach` and needs a nav test (`features/menu-navigation.md`). During the drive, touch
  players use `MobileControls.DEFAULT_SCHEME`.

### 4. The coin trail

A line of coins along the road from the start to the cabin, whose total is enough to buy any
other starter car at the cabin. Site 0 lights on arrival, so ring 0's stock (which holds the
starter cars, `LootRules.ring_tables`) is on sale there.

- **Not `RoadSites` coins.** Today's coins are one per pull-in or dead-end site (`LootDirector`,
  collected by site id into the world block's `collected`, and the site list is fingerprinted by
  `WorldSession.check_sites`). The trail is separate: `ColdOpen` owns it, lays it out from the
  start pose to the cabin, and it isn't saved. Coins not picked up vanish when the cold open ends.
- **Value from the catalogue, not a number.** Trail total = the highest `LootRules.price` of the
  other starter cars (`LootRules.starter_cars()` without the cold-open car), split evenly over
  `cold_open_trail_coins` coins (last coin takes the rounding). A retuned car price retunes the
  trail by itself.
- **Pick-up** goes through the existing money path: `WorldSession.set_money`, the pickup beep and
  the `LootDirector.said` toast ("+$N"), same as a road coin.
- **Look:** the road coin (`TerrainStreamer._coin_marker`, `coin.gdshader`). Pull its mesh and
  material out into a small builder both can call rather than copying it.
- The car can't give up during the cold open (the health floor), so the trail money can't be
  dropped at a wreck.

### 5. The only prompt: a throttle glyph

Until the player first presses throttle, show the accelerate binding as a glyph in the minimap's
prompt box (`MiniMap.toast`, as the rest-stop prompt does): the keyboard key or controller trigger
from `InputRemap`, matching the last device used. On touch, pulse the throttle pedal of
`MobileControls` instead of showing text. It goes away on the first press and never returns.

The HUD's existing cooling label ("OVERHEATING — REACH A CHECKPOINT") stays as it is; it's the one
line that names the goal.

### 6. Tunables

New `FreeRoamSettings` fields in a *Cold open* group (shipped values in `game_config.tres`, not in
scripts): `cold_open_car_id`, `cold_open_distance_m`, `cold_open_start_hp_frac`,
`cold_open_start_temp_frac`, `cold_open_hp_floor_frac`, `cold_open_trail_coins`. First guesses to tune by playing: ~300 m, HP just under
the cooling threshold, temperature ~80% of the overheat threshold, a floor around 10%.

## Docs and tests (with the change)

- Docs: `features/free-roam.md` (a *Cold open* section, the lamp post), `features/world-session.md`
  (the flag and boot order), `features/damage.md` (`min_hp`), `features/loot.md` (the trail),
  `features/menus.md` (first-run options after the drive),
  `features/README.md` index if a new file is added.
- Tests (logic only, no tuned values):
  - `apply_loss` never takes HP below `min_hp`, and `gave_up` never fires above it.
  - A fresh profile with the flag unset gets the cold open and no title; Continue, New World and a
    profile with the flag set get today's boot.
  - The cold-open start pose is on the road, inside the configured distance of site 0, facing it.
  - Lighting site 0 ends the cold open: floor cleared, weather override cleared, flag set, the
    first-run options shown, and no title menu after them.
  - The trail's total covers every other starter car's price (with `CarFixtures.install()`'s
    synthetic roster, not the real catalogue), and driving through a trail coin adds its value once.
  - The cold-open car is the configured id, with the fallback when the id is missing.
  - The first-run options page's keyboard and gamepad navigation.
  - The throttle glyph shows before the first accelerate press and never after.
  - Use `SceneTestHelpers.minimal_world()` / a synthetic setup where possible (`features/testing.md`).
