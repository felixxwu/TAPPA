## The finding: the note specified the MECHANISM, not the INVARIANT

This is the sharpest thing this round produced, and it only appears because the note worked well
enough to expose the next layer.

Round 018's note part 1 said: *"Author your own SURFACE terms, not a bare `tire_grip_mult`: one flat
multiplier is a strictly-weaker rung of the ladder race_tires tops."*

**The probe obeyed it exactly — three surface terms — and still shipped a strictly-weaker rung**,
because all three of its terms are penalties or below race's flat gain. The note named the *symptom*
(a bare multiplier) and the *mechanism* (use surface terms) but never stated the *property that
actually matters*:

> **A new compound must BEAT every sibling on at least one surface. Otherwise nobody will ever fit it.**

"Use three terms" is satisfiable without being non-dominated. That is a general lesson about writing
these notes, not a fact about tyres: **state the invariant the change must satisfy, and the check that
proves it — not the shape of the code that usually satisfies it.**

### The executable version is FORBIDDEN here, and that is worth recording

My first instinct was a guard: "every tyre-slot part must win on at least one surface." I am not
shipping it, because **`CLAUDE.md` explicitly bans it** — "asserting a monotonic/ordering relationship
across authored entries" is on the forbidden list, and the acid test ("would a designer retuning this
in the inspector break my test?") is plainly *yes*: a designer moving the gravel flat term to 1.10
would redden it.

So this is a case where §2.6's preference for an executable check over a note **collides with the
project's own testing rules, and the project's rules win.** The note is the only available lever, and
saying so is more honest than inventing a guard that violates the repo's conventions to satisfy a
process preference.

## Fix landed — part 1 now states the invariant and shows the arithmetic

`upgrade_library.gd`'s tyre-slot note, part 1, rewritten from "author surface terms, not a bare
multiplier" to:

- **the property, stated as the requirement:** "it must BEAT every sibling on at least one surface. A
