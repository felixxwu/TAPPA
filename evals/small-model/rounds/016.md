### CONFIRMED: T008 has been unwinnable-clean for six rounds because of a frozen assertion

| test | assertion | fixture |
|---|---|---|

`stars_gained` in the result is `rewards.stars_gained + bonus_stars`, so **the moment

**What round 010 did about it: it wrote a 22-line comment.** The seam now carries a block ending

out — which is why it is written here rather than left to be discovered."

That comment is well-written and correctly reasoned, and it is **still the wrong fix**, for a
reason the loop has already established twice:

- §2.5 names this cause exactly — "**a frozen assertion blocking a sanctioned change**" — and says
  it is **mine** to find, because "probes cannot discover this — they cannot run tests". Round 010
  put the discovery burden on the one party structurally unable to bear it.
- §2.6's ceiling rule: a note that has failed is not answered by a longer note. This one has now
  failed for **T008 in rounds 010 and (by construction) every round since**.

  unwinnable for three rounds for a reason with nothing to do with legibility. Round 011 finally

**Why the assertions are wrong on the project's own terms, not just inconvenient.** `CLAUDE.md`
forbids pinning a product choice or a tunable. `stars_gained == stars_for_placement(placed)` is an

an equality only true while a tunable sat at its default, and it reddened a sanctioned change a
round later." These two are that, unfixed.

**The invariant each test actually cares about** (and which must survive):

- the re-win test: a re-win pays stars *again*, grants *no* car and *no* new podium, and the
  balance moves by exactly what the result reported. The bare equality to `stars_for_placement(1)`
  is incidental to all four.
- the opening-rally test: a *losing* finish still pays **what finishing is worth, and more than
  nothing**. Again, the equality is incidental; `>=` plus `> 0` is the real claim.

**Planned fix (deferred until the T005 settlement probe returns, per §D2's no-code-edits-while-a-probe-is-out rule):**
relax both to `assert_gte` against the placement payout, keeping every other assertion untouched,
and then **delete the ~10 lines of the seam note that exist only to warn about the freeze** —
§2.6's "prefer deleting an obligation over describing it", and its instruction to "delete what
your addition makes redundant". `assert_gte` is available in this GUT vendor
(`addons/gut/test.gd:1002`) and already used elsewhere in the suite.

This is **not** weakening a test to reach green: nothing is red today. It is removing an assertion
that forbids a sanctioned feature, while preserving the invariant the test was written to protect
— which is what §2.5 requires of me.
