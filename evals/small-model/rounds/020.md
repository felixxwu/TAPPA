## Fix — restate the invariant symmetrically (fixing my own error, not the probe's)

`upgrade_library.gd`, part 1, now states the property in both directions and names the failure that
exposed it:

- **(a) your new part must WIN on at least one surface** — or it is a strictly-weaker rung;
- **(b) it must not TAKE the last surface from any existing part.** "If adding yours leaves a sibling
  winning nowhere, you have not removed a dead part, you have moved it — which is the same defect
  wearing a different name."
- the test restated as **"EVERY row in this slot wins somewhere", not just yours**;
- the observed failure named concretely ("An attempt satisfied (a) and broke (b) — it raised its flat
  term until it beat race_tires on gravel AND tarmac, leaving race_tires winning nothing at all");
- and the arithmetic guidance extended to explain *why* it happens: "A flat gain big enough to clear
  race_tires on gravel will also clear it on tarmac unless your tarmac term pays that gain back, so
  price the penalties against every surface, not just the one you care about."
