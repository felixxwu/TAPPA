## Fix A — CONFIRMED, and it worked better than "the test stayed green" suggests

The probe's own report says: *"it strengthens engine braking during both throttle lift-off AND foot
braking (**since `is_lifting_off()` is true while braking**)"*. That sentence is the corrected docstring
being read and understood — round 021's probe had no idea, and reddened `test_brake_lockup`.

Knowing it, this probe **scoped the change narrowly**: it built `friction_effective` and used it for
`crank` alone, leaving the `friction` variable itself untouched for every other consumer. Round 021's
probe instead restructured `friction` upstream, so the multiplier reached everything downstream of it.
**Same feature, same predicate, twice the gain (2.0 vs 1.5) — and green instead of red, because the
blast radius was scoped.** That is exactly what naming the consequence and the guard was supposed to buy.
