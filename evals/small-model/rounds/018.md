## Site-count analysis, done while attempt 1's probe was out (read-only)

`region_library.gd` already carries what is probably the most thorough point-of-use note in the
repo: a numbered four-part checklist, a **complete paste-and-edit rally template** with `# EDIT`
markers and a pre-computed legal `map_pos`, an explicit warning against the cheap wrong path
("Do NOT satisfy this by retagging an existing rally"), and the names of the guard tests that
enforce each part. Round 010's probe had all of that and still shipped parts 1 only.

Counting the **mandatory** sites honestly (part 4 is already conditional — "if the row changes how
terrain is built"):

| # | site | file | in-file or out? | already guarded? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | the `REGIONS` row | `region_library.gd` | **in-file** | asset-path resolver guard |
| 2 | a rally with `"region": "<id>"` | `rally_library.gd` | **out-of-file** | `test_every_region_is_reachable_from_at_least_one_rally` |
| 3 | the prose region list | `features/regions.md` | **out-of-file** | `test_region_docs.gd` |

**This is the uncomfortable finding, and it lands before the probe even returns: both out-of-file
obligations are ALREADY executable checks.** The repo is protected. Round 017's prescription
("convert the obligation into an executable check and stop spending rounds on the note") was
already applied here, rounds ago. So if attempt 1 truncates on parts 2–3 again, there is **no note
left to write and no guard left to add** — the remaining gap is exactly the harness limit (a probe
cannot run the tests that would tell it).

That leaves precisely one fix with teeth, and §2.6 names it:

> "When a point-of-use checklist grows past ~3 sites, **the checklist itself is the defect —
> collapse the sites.**"

**Design option A — let a region declare its own debut rally inline.** A `REGIONS` entry could carry
an optional `"debut_rally": {…}` block, with `region` filled in automatically from the row's id, and
`RallyLibrary` merging it into the roster. Adding a region would become a **one-file** edit and
reachability would be structurally guaranteed rather than remembered — §2.6a tier 2, "a registry
that must be extended for the feature to work at all beats any test, because there is nothing to
remember."

**The cost, stated honestly before I commit to it:** `RALLIES` is a `const` authored table with wide
consumption (map pins, `sim_career`, roster invariants, reward tiers, the eligibility query). Merging
synthetic entries into it is a **wide blast radius**, and this round runs under test mode `fast` —
no full-suite run. The skill is explicit that a correct fix carries its verification cost with it,
and that the alternative to shipping it unverified is to **defer it to a structural round**. I will
decide after grading attempt 1, and I will say which and why rather than drifting into a half-done
version of it.
