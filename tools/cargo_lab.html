Click a box or strap to select it, drag it to slide it over the car. Boxes rest on the body (or on a box listed above them); straps drape over whatever is under them.
Arrows: nudge 1 cm (Shift 10 cm); on a strap, ← → shorten / lengthen it (straps stay centred on the car) · Q / E: turn 5° (Shift 1°) · PgUp / PgDn: lift ±1 cm · Del: delete · D: duplicate · Esc: deselect.
